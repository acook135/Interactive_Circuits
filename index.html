How to use Circuit Lab

Building

  • Drag a carrier from the parts list onto the baseboard. It snaps between two pillars, and every carrier joined at a pillar is connected.
  • Press R while dragging, or after selecting a carrier, to rotate it.
  • Drag a placed carrier to move it. Select it and press Del to remove it.
  • Click a switch to change it. Press and hold the push switch.
  • Use the Component tab to change voltage, temperature, light level, materials and other settings.

Digital multimeter

  • Click a label around the dial to choose a mode: OFF, V⎓, Ω, continuity or A⎓.
  • Drag the red and black probes onto pillars. The probe tips lock on when you release them.
  • Voltage: place the probes across a component, in parallel.
  • Current: remove a link and place the probes in the gap so that the meter is in series. If you place the meter across a component in A mode, it shorts that component, just as a real meter would.
  • Resistance and continuity: isolate the supply first. The meter shows LIVE if it detects a voltage. Continuity beeps below 30 Ω.

Fault finding

Select Insert hidden fault to add a hidden open circuit or high-resistance joint to a random part. Use voltage-drop, continuity and resistance tests to find it.

Model notes

This is a teaching simulation. It uses simplified models: piecewise-linear diodes, a two-terminal potentiometer, a resistive phototransistor model and a 3 A supply trip.
